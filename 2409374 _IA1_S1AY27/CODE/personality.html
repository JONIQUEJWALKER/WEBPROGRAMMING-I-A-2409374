My Personality Traits

  1. Leisurely - doing something in a slow, relaxed way without any rush or pressure

  2. Self-critical - Often criticizes themselves, especially their own behaviour, work, or performance.

  3. Charismatic - Someone with a magnetic personality who naturally attracts, inspires, and influences.

My leisurely nature allows me to make it through college life without significant/excessive stress.
I believe the best work/focus can be done when your mind and body are as peaceful as it can be.

My self-criticalness allows me to hold myself accountable for my actions and responsibilities,
keeping focused on my classes and assignments.

My charismatic nature allows me to not only attract others, despite me usually being quiet.
But makes me surprisingly easy to have conversations with, despite my introverted nature.
