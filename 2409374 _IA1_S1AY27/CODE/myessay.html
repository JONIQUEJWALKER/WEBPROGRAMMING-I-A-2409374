Given the statement “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch”. Write a short critical essay/blog on what this statement means to you as a student. When where and how can LLMs be used in your studentship to enhance and improve your learning”. Provide reference to sources

The use of LLMS by students has only increased over the years with the creation of tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot.
With this, there has been an overreliance on these LLMs to the point that students are purely using these applications instead of
putting any effort into their own work. The statement “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot must be used as a tool, not a crutch”.
To me, as a student, this statement isn't demonizing or putting down the use of LLMs purely, but saying that it should be used as a tool to
aid in your work, assignments, and research rather than pure reliance. In the same way you would use a textbook or a search engine.
LLMs can be used by students as tools in many ways. For reading comprehension, “The capability of LLMS to transform texts may support active,
text-focused strategies such as paraphrasing and providing definitions of words and thereby help students to understand the language and meaning of
the text itself”(Kreijkes et al., 2025) Every student retains information in different ways and at different speeds. Some students work better reading
notes,some by powerpoint ,videos,audios or by worksheets and flashcards. “An LLM can adapt to this by providing more practice or explanation for students
who need it and offering advanced material for those who are ready to go further”(Admin and Admin)
